Out-of-fold predictions under record-grouped cross-validation: no record was ever scored by a model that saw it. The only label is cord pH at birth, so a value here is “given what is known at this minute, this baby ends acidaemic with probability p”. Discrimination falls from about 0.68 at delivery to near chance an hour out — read early positions as explanation, not as warning. Odds ratios are measured against a typical birth in the reference population you select; the absolute probability rests on that assumption, the ratio does not. Cohort: CTU-CHB, 512 records with clinician annotations, 32% acidaemic.
